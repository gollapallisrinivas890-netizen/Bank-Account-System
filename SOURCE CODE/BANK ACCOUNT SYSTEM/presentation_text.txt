ABSTRACT The  Bank Account Management System  is a C++-based application designed to simulate basic banking operations in a simple and organized environment. The system allows users to create and manage bank accounts and perform operations such as  depositing money, withdrawing money, checking account balance, and viewing account details.  The main purpose of the project is to provide a  simple and user-friendly environment for understanding banking operations, account management, transaction handling, and basic financial activities

=== slide4.xml ===
INTRODUCTION The  Bank Account Management System  is a C++-based application developed to manage basic banking activities in a simple and organized way. It allows users to create bank accounts, store customer details, and perform common operations such as  deposit, withdrawal, balance enquiry, and viewing account details . It provides a  simple and user-friendly approach  to handling banking operations and demonstrates the practical implementation of  Object-Oriented Programming concepts in C++ , such as classes, objects, data members, and member functions.

=== slide5.xml ===
PROBLEM STATEMENT Manual management of bank accounts can be time-consuming and may cause errors in maintaining customer details and transactions. A simple and user-friendly system is needed to manage  account creation, deposits, withdrawals, balance enquiry, and account details  efficiently.

=== slide6.xml ===
PROPOSED SOLUTION Develop a  C++ Bank Account Management System  that provides account creation, customer details management, deposit, withdrawal, balance enquiry, and account information management. The system will maintain account records and transaction details for each customer Thus, the system helps users understand  banking operations, account management, transaction handling, and basic financial activities  in a simple, organized, and practical way.

MODULES/FUNCTIONALITIES Login Module  – Provides secure access to authorized users. Account Creation  – Creates new customer bank accounts. Account Details  – Stores and displays customer and account information. Deposit  – Adds money to the customer’s account. Withdrawal  – Allows users to withdraw available money. Balance Enquiry  – Shows the current account balance. Search Account  – Finds accounts using account number or customer name. Update Account  – Modifies customer or account details. Transaction Module  – Records deposits and withdrawals. File Storage  – Saves account and transaction records in files.
